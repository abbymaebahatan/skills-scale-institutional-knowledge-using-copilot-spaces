# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Specialist

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Specialists ensure product quality through comprehensive test planning, execution, and validation. They collaborate with developers and product managers to define acceptance criteria and verify features meet quality standards before release. They are critical partners in the execution and release phases, working closely with the development team to maintain quality gates.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated testing (unit, integration, end-to-end)
- Validate acceptance criteria are met before marking work complete
- Identify and document defects with clear reproduction steps
- Participate in release readiness verification and smoke testing
- Provide quality metrics and test coverage reports
- Collaborate with developers on testability and test automation strategy

### Goals
- Ensure product quality and user satisfaction
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Maintain high test coverage and enable confident releases
- Enable fast, reliable feedback loops for developers

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Defect reports with reproduction steps and severity levels
- QA standup updates on testing progress and blockers
- Release readiness checklists and quality sign-offs
- Quality metrics dashboards and coverage reports

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with **Developers** to clarify acceptance criteria, discuss test strategy, and validate implementations
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** to understand feature intent and success criteria
- Supports **Project Managers** with QA timelines, blockers, and release readiness assessments
- Reports to **Product Lead** on quality metrics and release fitness

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approve scope and resource allocation, and ensure project outcomes align with organizational strategy. They may have authority over budget, timeline decisions, and go/no-go approvals. Stakeholders serve as the voice of business priorities and organizational constraints in project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business requirements and success criteria
- Approve project charter and scope baseline
- Allocate budget and resources
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates
- Provide feedback on deliverables and priorities
- Communicate organizational constraints and dependencies
- Serve as escalation point for significant risks or issues
- Validate that outcomes deliver expected business value

### Goals
- Maximize business value and ROI
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy
- Manage risk and resource trade-offs effectively
- Enable timely decision-making and risk escalation

### Typical Communication
- Project status reports and milestone reviews
- Decision memos and approval documentation
- Escalations for scope, timeline, or resource changes
- Budget and resource allocation discussions
- Go/no-go decision meetings at key project gates

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with **Project Manager** on timeline, scope, and risk escalations
- Aligns with **Product Manager** on business priorities and success metrics
- Provides governance and approval authority to all delivery team members
- Receives escalations from **Product Lead** on major strategic decisions

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide strategic product direction and business oversight. They work closely with Product Managers to ensure features align with product vision, coordinate across multiple projects or teams, and serve as the primary escalation point for product decisions. Product Leads bridge product strategy and execution, and often serve as the escalation path for cross-team dependencies.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate product vision and strategy
- Review and approve product roadmap and prioritization
- Escalate product-level decisions and trade-offs
- Coordinate across dependent teams and projects
- Validate that solutions meet business goals and user needs
- Drive user research and market feedback into planning
- Approve release notes and customer-facing communication
- Make trade-off decisions between competing priorities across projects

### Goals
- Ensure product coherence and market fit
- Maximize cross-team alignment and reduce friction
- Drive measurable business outcomes
- Maintain user satisfaction and product quality standards

### Typical Communication
- Strategy documents and product roadmap reviews
- Prioritization meetings with stakeholders and PMs
- Stakeholder briefings on product direction
- Product guidance and decision memos
- Escalation meetings for cross-team dependencies

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Mentors and aligns with **Product Managers** on strategy and priorities
- Escalates strategic decisions to **Stakeholders/Sponsors**
- Partners with **Project Manager** on cross-team dependencies and release coordination
- Reviews quality and release readiness input from **QA/Testing Specialist**
- Provides technical direction oversight with **Technical Lead** (if applicable)

---

## Technical Lead (Optional)

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction and architecture guidance for complex projects. They mentor developers, make key technical decisions, and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical strategy. Technical Leads serve as the voice of technical excellence and long-term maintainability in project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Provide technical design guidance and architecture input
- Review technical proposals and designs
- Mentor team members on technical best practices
- Make trade-off decisions on technical complexity vs. time
- Ensure adherence to coding standards and technical policies
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Advocate for technical debt paydown and infrastructure improvements
- Collaborate with QA to ensure testability and quality gates

### Goals
- Ensure technical excellence and code quality
- Reduce technical risk and future maintenance burden
- Accelerate team delivery through effective architecture
- Maintain code quality, maintainability, and observability standards

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decision records
- Architecture decisions and technical trade-offs
- Code review guidance and mentorship
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation plans
- Technical debt and infrastructure improvement proposals

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Guides **Developers** on technical decisions and best practices
- Partners with **Project Manager** on technical risks and timelines
- Collaborates with **QA/Testing Specialist** on test architecture and automation strategy
- Provides technical input to **Product Lead** on feasibility and trade-offs
- Escalates significant technical risks to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** via **Product Lead**
